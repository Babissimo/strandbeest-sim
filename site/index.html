Four-bar linkage

Four sticks joined in a loop: a fixed ground, a crank that turns, a coupler and a rocker. Change their lengths and watch the path drawn by a point on the coupler.

Jansen leg

Theo Jansen's leg, with every length in mm. Change any of them and see how the foot moves: how long its stride is, how high it lifts, and whether the leg jams. The body is held still here, so the foot moves backwards along the ground while the beest walks forwards.

Lengths in mm, hole to hole

Evolve a beest

A genetic algorithm breeds whole beests. In each generation the beests that score best are the most likely to pass their lengths on to the next. Try hill climbing and random search too, with the same number of evaluations, and compare them on the graph.
