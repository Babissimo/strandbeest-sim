Evolve a beest

A genetic algorithm breeds whole beests. In each generation the beests that score best are the most likely to pass their lengths on to the next. Try hill climbing and random search too, with the same number of evaluations, and compare them on the graph. To score and mutate the beests with your own Python, choose my code.

Arena

A whole beest on the ground, with gravity, friction and the weight of every stick. Its feet can slip and it can fall over, which the scores in Evolve leave out. The crank turns once a second, as a motor would turn it.

The beest

Each pair is a leg and its mirror image on one throw of the crank. The throws are spread evenly round the crank. The best from Evolve is the best beest of its whole run so far.

Race

The distance race lasts 10 turns. The pencils and the towel are crossed once every foot is past the far side, and a beest still not across after 20 turns gives up. A beest that falls over is out.
