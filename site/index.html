Four-bar linkage

Four sticks joined in a loop: a fixed ground, a crank that turns, a coupler and a rocker. Change their lengths and watch the path drawn by a point on the coupler.

Jansen leg

Theo Jansen's leg, with every length in mm. Change any of them and see how the foot moves: how long its stride is, how high it lifts, and whether the leg jams.

Evolve a beest

A genetic algorithm breeds whole beests. In each generation the beests that score best are the most likely to pass their lengths on to the next.
